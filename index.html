Formula 1 - Car & Race Rules

Formula 1 is one of the fastest and most advanced forms of motorsport in the world.

Every F1 car is designed according to strict rules created to make racing safe and fair.

Rules for Designing an F1 Car

  • The car must follow FIA technical regulations.
  • The dimensions of the car must stay within the allowed limits.
  • The aerodynamic bodywork must follow specific regulations.
  • The front and rear wings must follow FIA requirements.
  • The car must use the tyres specified for Formula 1.
  • The power unit must follow the current engine regulations.
  • The car must meet the minimum weight requirement.
  • The car must have safety structures to protect the driver.

F1 Race Rules

  • Drivers must stay within the track limits.
  • Drivers must obey all flags shown by race officials.
  • Overtaking must be performed safely.
  • Drivers must slow down when required by yellow flags.
  • A red flag can stop a race because of dangerous conditions.
  • Drivers must follow the pit-lane speed limit.
  • Drivers can receive penalties for breaking the rules.
  • Championship points are awarded according to finishing position.
